## Description

Make the time until clock-out feel a little lighter.

ClockJoy turns your workday into visible progress. Set your schedule and pay details to see an estimate of today’s earnings, track how far you are through the day, and count down to clock-out with a playful companion by your side.

SEE TODAY MOVE FORWARD
• Follow estimated earnings during your scheduled work hours
• Check today’s progress and time remaining at a glance
• Review monthly and yearly summaries
• Record overtime days and early clock-outs

KEEP IT CLOSE
• Add Home Screen widgets
• Follow the countdown from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
• Sync your preferences privately with iCloud when enabled

MAKE PROGRESS FEEL PERSONAL
• Choose themes, rooms, and companion styles
• Turn earnings progress into small goals and rewards
• Create share cards that hide real amounts by default

ClockJoy is designed for motivation and playful visualization. Earnings and time calculations are estimates based on the information you enter; they are not payroll records, financial advice, or legal advice.
