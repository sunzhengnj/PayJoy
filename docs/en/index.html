iPhone · clock-out countdown · estimated earnings

Make the workday feel like it is actually moving.

ClockJoy shows today’s estimated earnings, a countdown to clock-out, and Lock Screen widgets. It does not ask you to love work. It helps you finish the day.

Download on the App Store See if it fits

Free to start. PRO is a one-time purchase. Amounts are estimates from your settings, not a paycheck.

See today move forward

After you set hours and pay, the home screen shows estimated earnings, a progress bar, time remaining, and how much is left to earn today.

Keep the countdown close

Add a Home Screen widget or follow Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. You do not need to keep opening the app.

Put a small wish into the day

Progress on a wish is manual. Each finished workday can move it a little closer. No streak pressure, no payroll math.

What it is not

For

People who want a visible way through a long shift: a countdown, estimated earnings, and a quieter Lock Screen companion.

Not for

Timesheets, overtime law, payroll proof, invoicing, or tax filing. Use a dedicated payroll or hours tracker for those jobs.
