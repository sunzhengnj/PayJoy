How to use the Lock Screen countdown

ClockJoy can sit in three places: a Home Screen widget, a Lock Screen Live Activity, and Dynamic Island. Opening the app shows a fresher estimate. System surfaces refresh on iOS’s schedule.

Before you start

Set work hours and pay inside ClockJoy. The countdown has nothing to compute without those two inputs.

Add a widget

  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen, then tap Edit or +.
  2. Search for ClockJoy.
  3. Choose a size and place it on the Home Screen or Today View.

Widgets are not per-second timers. That limit applies to every iOS widget.

Live Activities

Start a Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity from the app while a shift is in progress. When the day ends or you stop the activity, it leaves the system surfaces.

Hide the amount

If the phone sits face-up at a desk, turn on privacy mode so widgets and Live Activities do not show the dollar figure. Share cards hide real amounts by default.
