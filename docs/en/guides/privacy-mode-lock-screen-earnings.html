How ClockJoy privacy mode fits Lock Screen intent

ClockJoy is a playful workday progress app. You enter your schedule and optional pay; during those hours it estimates how far you are through the day—and optionally how much of today's scheduled pay you've earned so far—then surfaces that on:

  • Lock Screen Live Activities for time-to-clock-out (see clock-out Live Activity)
  • Dynamic Island on supported iPhones
  • Home Screen widgets for progress and optional estimated earnings
  • Privacy mode so amounts stay hidden on those surfaces when the desk is not private
  • Share cards that hide amounts by default when you post a progress moment

You can also note overtime or early clock-out for personal context, track wish/goal progress manually, and switch themes or companions. English UI ships with Japanese and Korean support as well. Estimates always come from pay and schedule you enter—never from your bank, HR portal, or payroll system. Estimates ≠ paycheck.
