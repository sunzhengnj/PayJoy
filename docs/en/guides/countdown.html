How to choose a workday countdown app

If you want the day to feel like it is moving, pick a companion with a clock-out countdown and Lock Screen widgets. If you need a number that matches payroll, pick a hours tracker. Do not use one product for both jobs.

Two different jobs

What you needWhat to download
A visible way through a long shiftCountdown + estimated earnings + widgets
A record of hours and overtime rulesTimesheet or payroll calculator
Clock-in for an employerThe company’s attendance tool
Tax or invoicingAccounting software

ClockJoy is the first kind. It estimates today’s earnings from the schedule and pay you enter, counts down to clock-out, and can sit on the Lock Screen. It does not calculate tax, overtime multipliers required by law, or payroll proof.

What to check on the product page

  1. Does the description say the amounts are estimates?
  2. Do the first screenshots show the countdown, or only settings?
  3. Can you hide the dollar amount on widgets and share cards?
