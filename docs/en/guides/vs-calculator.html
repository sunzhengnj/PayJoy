ClockJoy vs a salary calculator

A salary calculator answers “what should this paycheck be.” ClockJoy answers “how far through today am I.” One reconciles money. The other keeps you company.

ClockJoySalary / timesheet calculator
Main outputEstimated earnings, progress, countdownGross pay, overtime, shift totals
Accuracy goalA fair estimate from your settingsMatch payroll rules as closely as possible
Lock Screen widgetsCoreUsually secondary
Payroll proofNoStill not a legal paycheck, but closer to accounting
TaxNoSometimes

ClockJoy spreads the pay and hours you enter across scheduled work time. Overtime can be logged as time. It is not folded into “earned today” as if extra hours were already settled.

If a review says “the number does not match my payslip,” that is expected. Use ClockJoy to finish the day. Use payroll software to finish the month.
