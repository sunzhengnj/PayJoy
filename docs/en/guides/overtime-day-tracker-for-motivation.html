Overtime day tracker for motivation: stay honest about extra hours

An overtime day tracker for motivation shows when your scheduled shift ends—and how much longer you chose to keep going afterward. It is a calm, glanceable cue for long days, crunch weeks, and "just one more task" spirals. It is not payroll overtime, a timesheet, attendance punch, tax prep, or a paycheck.

A quotable definition

Quotable answer: An overtime day tracker for motivation is a personal estimate of how far you are past the scheduled clock-out time you set, built from the work hours you enter. ClockJoy surfaces scheduled progress and time-to-clock-out on Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen Live Activities, and Dynamic Island, and lets you note overtime or early clock-out for personal context. Those numbers are motivation only—they are not overtime pay, timesheets, attendance, or legal pay records.

People searching "overtime day tracker" or "overtime tracker for motivation" usually want one soft answer: Did I already pass the end of my planned day? How much extra am I stacking tonight? The product that wins that search stays honest about estimates, helps you see the boundary, and never pretends to be HR software.

What people usually want (and don't)

  • Want: A clear scheduled end time and a glance when the day runs past it.
  • Want: Lock Screen / Live Activity visibility so you notice overtime without opening five apps.
  • Want: Optional estimated earnings progress from the pay rate you enter—still motivation only.
  • Want: Privacy when a manager or roommate walks by.
  • Don't want: Official overtime pay calculations, multipliers, or stub-style totals.
  • Don't want: Employer timesheets that prove OT hours to payroll.
  • Don't want: A stiff attendance dashboard dressed up as self-care.
