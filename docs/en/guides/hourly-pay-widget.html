Hourly pay widget for iPhone: estimated earnings at a glance

An hourly pay widget on iPhone shows a running estimate of today’s scheduled earnings so the workday feels like it is moving. ClockJoy is built for that glance—Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Dynamic Island—not for payroll proof, timesheets, tax, or attendance.

What an hourly pay widget actually does

People search for “hourly pay widget” when they want a visible sense of progress: how far they are through the shift, and a rough sense of money earned so far. The useful product for that job takes your schedule and pay inputs, estimates today’s total, and surfaces a countdown or progress number where you already look—especially the Lock Screen.

That is different from logging every minute for an employer, applying overtime multipliers required by law, or producing a number that must match a paycheck. If you need a record that HR or accounting will accept, use a timesheet or payroll tool. If you need the day to feel lighter, use a countdown companion with estimated earnings.

How ClockJoy’s hourly-style estimate works

  1. Enter your work hours and pay details inside the app.
  2. During the scheduled day, ClockJoy estimates how much of today’s pay you have “earned” so far and how much time remains until clock-out.
  3. Add a Home Screen widget, or start a Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity, so you can check progress without unlocking a spreadsheet.
  4. Use privacy mode when you do not want the dollar amount visible on a desk.

Widget and Live Activity refresh follow iOS rules. Opening the app always shows the freshest estimate; system surfaces update on the schedule Apple allows, not as a per-second payroll ticker.

Who this is for

  • Hourly and salaried workers who want a playful countdown through a long shift
  • Freelancers who want a day-earnings estimate for motivation (not invoicing)
  • Anyone who already checked a Lock Screen timer and wished it also showed “how far through pay today”

Who it is not for: employers needing attendance; accountants needing stubs; anyone who must treat the on-screen dollar amount as legal pay.

Setup checklist

  1. Install ClockJoy from the App Store (id6771261514).
  2. Set schedule and pay so the estimate has inputs.
  3. Touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit / + → search ClockJoy → place a widget.
  4. Optionally start a Live Activity for Lock Screen and Dynamic Island during a shift.

FAQ

What is an hourly pay widget on iPhone?

It is a Home Screen or Lock Screen glance that estimates how much of today’s scheduled pay you have earned so far from hours and rates you enter. It motivates; it does not replace payroll.

Does ClockJoy calculate overtime required by law?

No. You can note overtime-style days for personal motivation, but ClockJoy does not apply jurisdiction-specific overtime multipliers or produce a legal OT calculation.

Will the widget match my paycheck?

Not reliably, and it is not designed to. Breaks, unpaid time, tips, bonuses, tax, and employer rules live outside a personal estimate. Use payroll systems for the official number.
