Quotable answer: A freelancer day-earnings estimate is a personal estimate of earnings progress during the scheduled hours you set for today, built from the pay rate and schedule you enter. FAQ

What is a freelancer day-earnings estimate?

It is a personal glance at how much of today's scheduled pay you've earned so far during work blocks you set—from the rate and schedule you enter. Motivation only; not an invoice, tax figure, timesheet, or paycheck.

Is a day-earnings estimate the same as invoicing or a timesheet?

No. Invoices and timesheets record billable work for payment. A day-earnings estimate only tracks personal progress through hours you chose for today.

Can ClockJoy replace my invoice, tax, or client timesheet tools?

No. ClockJoy's figures are motivation estimates from your inputs. Keep official tools for billing and taxes.
