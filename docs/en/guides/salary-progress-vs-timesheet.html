Salary progress vs timesheet: motivation estimate or payroll record?

Salary progress is a personal estimate of how far you are through today’s scheduled workday—and optionally how much of today’s scheduled pay you have “earned” so far. A timesheet is a record of hours meant for employers, clients, or payroll. ClockJoy is built for the first job: glanceable motivation on widgets and Live Activities. It is not a timesheet, payroll system, tax tool, or attendance punch.

What “salary progress” means in plain language

People search for salary progress when the workday feels endless and they want a visible sense of movement: percent of the shift done, time left until clock-out, or a rough running estimate of today’s pay. That estimate usually comes from a schedule and rate you enter yourself. The point is pacing and morale—not producing a number that must match a paycheck.

Quotable answer: ClockJoy estimates today’s workday and optional earnings progress from the schedule and pay inputs you enter, then shows countdown and progress on Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen, and Dynamic Island Live Activities. Those figures are motivation estimates only—they are not payroll, timesheet, tax, or attendance records.

How ClockJoy shows salary / workday progress

  1. Enter your scheduled work hours and optional pay details inside ClockJoy.
  2. During the scheduled day, ClockJoy estimates time remaining and how far you are through today’s plan—optionally with estimated earnings so far.
  3. Add a Home Screen widget, or start a Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity, so progress is one glance away.
  4. Use privacy mode when you do not want dollar amounts visible on a shared desk. Share cards hide amounts by default.

Widget and Live Activity refresh follow iOS rules. Opening the app always shows the freshest estimate; system surfaces update on the schedule Apple allows. See also clock-out Live Activity and Dynamic Island countdown.

When to use each tool

  • Use a timesheet when someone else needs a defensible record of hours—payroll, billing, compliance, or attendance.
  • Use salary / workday progress when you need a personal boundary cue: end-of-shift countdown, percent complete, or a soft earnings glance for motivation.
  • Use both if you punch into a company system and want a private Lock Screen companion that is not your HR stack.

ClockJoy does not apply jurisdiction-specific overtime multipliers or produce legal OT math. You can note long days for personal logging; official overtime still lives in payroll tools.

Who this comparison is for

  • Knowledge workers who confuse “hours left today” with another productivity dashboard
  • Hourly and salaried people who want estimated day progress without treating it as a stub
  • Anyone comparing motivation apps to timesheet or payroll products
  • Users who want English UI with Japanese and Korean support as well

Who it is not for: employers needing attendance; accountants needing stubs; anyone who must treat an on-screen estimate as legal pay.

FAQ

What is salary progress?

It is a personal estimate of how far you are through today’s scheduled pay or workday—often a percentage, countdown, or running earnings glance. It motivates; it does not replace payroll.

Is salary progress the same as a timesheet?

No. A timesheet records hours for an employer, client, or payroll system. ClockJoy’s progress views estimate the day from the schedule and pay you enter for personal pacing only.

Can I use ClockJoy instead of my company’s timesheet?

No. Use official company tools for clock-in and pay. Use ClockJoy for Lock Screen, widget, and Dynamic Island motivation glances.
