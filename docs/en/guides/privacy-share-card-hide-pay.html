Privacy share card: share workday progress without showing pay

A privacy-first share card lets you celebrate how far you got through today’s scheduled hours—progress bar, clock-out countdown, soft vibe—while estimated pay amounts stay hidden by default. ClockJoy builds those estimates from the pay and schedule you entered, then defaults share cards to amount-free so a story, chat, or post never broadcasts dollars. It is motivation sharing. It is not a pay stub, payroll screenshot, timesheet, tax form, or attendance proof. Estimates ≠ paycheck.

How ClockJoy privacy share defaults work

ClockJoy is a playful workday progress app. You enter your schedule and optional pay; during those hours it estimates how far through the day you are and, if you like, how much of today’s scheduled pay you’ve earned so far. When you want to share a moment:

  • Share cards hide amounts by default — progress and clock-out cues stay; estimated dollars stay off until you choose otherwise.
  • Privacy mode pairs with Lock Screen — amounts stay soft on widgets, Live Activities, and Dynamic Island too (see privacy mode Lock Screen earnings).
  • Estimates stay local to your inputs — never synced from payroll, banks, or HR portals.
  • Overtime / early clock-out notes — personal motivation context, not OT pay math.
  • Themes and companions — playful cards, not stiff SaaS dumps.

Around it you still get Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activities, a clock-out countdown, monthly/yearly summaries, and wish/goal progress. English UI ships with Japanese and Korean support too. Estimates ≠ paycheck.

Setup checklist before you share a progress card

  1. Confirm amounts are hidden on the card preview—default should already be amount-free.
  2. Ask: am I sharing mood/progress, or accidentally implying real pay?
  3. Keep Lock Screen privacy on if the phone sits face-up at an open desk.
  4. Treat any number you do reveal as an estimate from what you entered—never as a stub.
  5. When someone needs real pay proof, send the payroll portal—not a ClockJoy card.

FAQ

What is a privacy share card for workday progress?

A shareable snapshot of progress and time-to-clock-out with estimated pay amounts hidden by default. Motivation sharing only—not a paycheck, tax figure, timesheet, or attendance record.

Is a ClockJoy share card the same as posting a pay stub screenshot?

No. Stubs show employer pay and deductions. ClockJoy cards hide amounts by default and only reflect estimates from inputs you entered. Estimates ≠ paycheck.

How is a share card different from Lock Screen privacy mode?

Lock Screen privacy is for glances on your phone. A share card is what you export or post—same hide-amounts default, different surface (feed/chat vs desk).
