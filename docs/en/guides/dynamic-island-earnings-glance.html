Dynamic Island earnings glance: what updates, what iOS controls

A Dynamic Island earnings glance is a Live Activity peek at optional estimated workday earnings—and often time left or progress—while you use other apps on a supported iPhone. ClockJoy computes that estimate from the pay and schedule you entered during scheduled hours. It is not a payroll feed, timesheet punch, tax calculator, or attendance system. Estimates ≠ paycheck. iOS—not the app alone—decides how often the Island refreshes and whether Live Activities are allowed at all.

A quotable definition

Quotable answer: ClockJoy’s Dynamic Island earnings glance is a Live Activity that may show estimated earnings (and countdown / progress) based on your scheduled hours and the pay rate you typed in. What ClockJoy updates

During a scheduled work block, ClockJoy can drive a Live Activity that may include:

  • Optional estimated earnings — progress through the day from pay and schedule you entered only. Estimates ≠ paycheck.
  • Clock-out countdown / workday progress — how far through the plan you are (same job as the progress bar and clock-out Live Activity guides).
  • Privacy-aware presentation — amounts can stay hidden while the day still feels measurable.

Nothing here is pulled from your employer’s payroll API. If pay or hours change in real life, update what you entered in ClockJoy—the glance only knows what you told it.

What iOS controls

  1. Live Activities permission — ClockJoy needs Live Activities allowed in Settings; otherwise the Island never hosts the activity.
  2. Refresh cadence — Apple decides how often Live Activities and Dynamic Island update. Expect glanceable progress, not a stock-style per-second ticker. Opening the app is the reliable way to see the freshest estimate.
  3. Low Power Mode & system load — iOS may throttle background and Live Activity updates when the battery or device is constrained.
  4. Focus / Do Not Disturb and device support — Focus settings and whether your iPhone has Dynamic Island affect what you see and when.
  5. Activity lifecycle — When the scheduled day ends or you end the activity, it leaves system surfaces.

If the Island looks “stuck,” check permission, that a shift Live Activity is actually running, device support, and power/Focus—then open ClockJoy for the current estimate before assuming a bug.

Privacy on a public desk

Estimated dollars on Dynamic Island or Lock Screen can be visible to whoever glances at your phone. Use privacy mode so progress stays useful while amounts stay hidden by default—same spirit as privacy mode Lock Screen earnings and privacy-safe share cards. Desk privacy is for you; it is not a substitute for payroll redaction policies at work.
