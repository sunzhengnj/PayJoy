# LinkedIn — Workday countdown as a boundary tool for knowledge workers

English only. Professional tone. Soft boundary framing first. Light ClockJoy CTA. UI supports Japanese and Korean. Do not publish until asked.

## Post

Knowledge work rarely has a clean “shift end.” Slack stays open. One more review. The day dissolves.

A simple workday countdown can be a boundary tool—not another productivity dashboard. One glance that answers: how much of today’s scheduled day is left?

That is different from a timesheet. A timesheet records hours for employers and payroll. A personal countdown estimates progress from the schedule you set, so you can close the laptop when the day you planned is done.

**ClockJoy** is a playful workday companion for that second job: countdown, optional estimated earnings, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activities, and widgets. Estimates only — not payroll, a timesheet, tax, or attendance.
