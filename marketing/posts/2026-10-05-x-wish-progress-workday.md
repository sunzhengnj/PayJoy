# X thread — Wish progress: let workdays move a small goal closer

## Post

1/
Small trick for long workdays: give the day a wish.

Not "save $X by Q4." Something tiny. New headphones. A weekend trip. A pottery class.

Then watch each workday nudge it closer.

2/
In ClockJoy you add a wish by typing it, pasting a link, or dropping in a photo.

Set an optional target and it shows about how many workdays to go, based on the hours and pay you entered. No price? Just slide progress yourself.

3/
What it is not: a bank, a budget, or a paycheck. Nothing moves real money. Estimates are motivation only.

No streaks either. Pause a wish, let it go, or mark it fulfilled and keep it on your wish shelf.

4/
ClockJoy is a playful workday progress app for iPhone, with widgets, a Lock Screen countdown, and wish progress. English UI, plus Japanese and Korean.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6771261514
Guide: https://sunzhengnj.github.io/PayJoy/en/guides/wish-goal-progress-workday.html

What's your current little wish?
