# X — Put end-of-shift on Lock Screen (stop opening Clock)

Post from **@ycbhsz**. English only. Soft tip first. Light ClockJoy CTA. UI supports Japanese and Korean.

## Post

Stop opening the Clock app just to see when your shift ends.

Put end-of-shift on your Lock Screen instead—one glance when you wake the phone. No calendar clutter. No to-do list. Just “how much of today is left?”

**ClockJoy** does that with a workday countdown (and optional estimated earnings) on Lock Screen and Live Activity. Estimates only — not payroll, a timesheet, tax, or attendance.
