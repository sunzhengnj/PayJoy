# Instagram — Privacy share card: progress without flashing pay

## Post

Sharing rule I actually keep:

1. Progress stays — how far through today's scheduled hours.
2. Countdown stays — time left until the clock-out I set.
3. Dollars hide — share cards keep estimated pay off by default.
4. Mood over money — "almost done" without a stub vibe.

I like posting a soft workday moment. I just don't want Stories or group chat broadcasting pay. ClockJoy estimates progress from the hours and pay I enter—and privacy share cards stay amount-free until I choose otherwise.
