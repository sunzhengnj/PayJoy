# Threads — clock-out

## Post (paste)

The best workday widget I’ve found isn’t a to-do list.

It’s a countdown to clock-out on the Lock Screen.

Glance. See how much of the shift is left. Close the phone. Keep going until it hits zero.

If you also show estimated earnings, keep it honest: personal estimate only — not payroll, not a timesheet, not tax, not attendance.

That’s what **ClockJoy** is for on iPhone.
