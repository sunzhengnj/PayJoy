# Instagram — Dynamic Island earnings glance (not a payroll ticker)

## Post

That little Island peek is not a paycheck ticker.

1. Optional estimated earnings — from hours and pay you typed in.
2. Often with time left / progress — soft “how far through today?”
3. iOS decides refresh — Live Activities, Focus, Low Power. Open the app for the freshest number.
4. Hide amounts when the desk is public — progress can stay; dollars can wait.

ClockJoy on Dynamic Island is a Live Activity for motivation. Estimates are not a paycheck. Not a timesheet. Not tax. Not attendance.
