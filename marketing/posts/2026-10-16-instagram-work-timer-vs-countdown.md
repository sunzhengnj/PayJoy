# Instagram — Work timer vs countdown: different jobs

## Post

Two apps. Two jobs. Don't mix them up.

1. Work timer — "How long have I already worked?" Start. Stop. Billable hours.
2. Countdown — "How far through today's plan? When do I clock out?"
3. Progress bar — soft glance, not a punch.
4. Estimates — optional mood math from hours you entered. Not a stub.

I keep a timer for client work. I keep ClockJoy for the end of the day I planned—widgets, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island. Progress and time left. Not a timesheet.

Estimates are not a paycheck. Not a timesheet. Not tax. Not attendance. Not billable proof.

ClockJoy — playful workday progress for iPhone. Countdown, not a stopwatch. English UI, with Japanese and Korean support too.
